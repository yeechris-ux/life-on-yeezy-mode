About

Two lives, one operating system.

I spent my twenties as a CPA reading other people’s balance sheets, then built two beverage brands — Brew Your Bucha and The Cocktail Box Co. — past $2.8M in revenue and sold both in 2021. Retiring at 35 sounds like an ending. It was mostly a very quiet room and a lot of questions.

What filled it was training. I coach runners now, I’m building toward a sub-2:45 marathon and an Ironman, and I travel with my family between blocks. Yeezy Mode is what I call going all in on the thing in front of you — and this site is the honest record of it, wins and blowups included.
